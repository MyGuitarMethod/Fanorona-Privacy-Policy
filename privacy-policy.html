Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how MinnyMedia handles information in Fanorona, the Android board game, and on this privacy-policy website. Android package: com.minnymedia.fanorona.

Fanorona stores campaign progress on your device. The current release does not include advertising or automatically send gameplay data to MinnyMedia. The advertising provisions below apply to releases in which Google AdMob is enabled.

Game progress stored on your device

Fanorona saves campaign results, tutorial progress, and earned boards and piece sets locally so you can continue playing. You do not need a Fanorona account. MinnyMedia does not operate a server that receives these local game records.

You can delete local progress using Android Settings to clear Fanorona's app storage. This resets your progress and unlocks. Device backup or restore behavior is controlled by Android and your device settings; MinnyMedia does not operate a cloud-save service.

Google AdMob advertising, when enabled

Ad-enabled releases use Google's AdMob service and Google Mobile Ads SDK. When the advertising SDK is active, Google may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available and permitted.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, crashes, and technical errors.

Google uses this information for ad delivery and personalization where permitted, advertising measurement and analytics, service performance, security, and fraud prevention. Collection depends on the SDK, device settings, consent choices, and the advertising configuration. Non-personalized advertising may still involve technical data and identifiers for measurement and fraud prevention.

In ad-enabled releases, applicable consent and privacy choices are presented where required before advertising data is processed. Where available, use the app's privacy options to review or change your choice. You can also manage advertising privacy and advertising identifiers in your Android settings. Available controls depend on your Android version and region.

For Google's handling of advertising information, including retention, international processing, and privacy controls, see Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services, and Google's advertising information.

Contacting MinnyMedia

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and resolve support or privacy requests. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your support messages or local campaign records.

Storage, security, and your choices

Local game records remain on your device until cleared or otherwise removed. Their security also depends on your device's access controls. Advertising data is handled by Google under its policies and retention practices; clearing Fanorona's storage does not itself delete data already held by Google.

You may contact MinnyMedia to ask about information you have provided to us or request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law. For information held by Google, use Google's privacy controls and request procedures. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

Fanorona does not ask players to provide a name, email address, or date of birth to play. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to MinnyMedia, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Advertising in any release remains subject to the applicable age-related settings, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

This website and external links

This privacy-policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, or contact forms. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. External websites and services handle information under their own policies.
